## Interview Preparation Tips

### Technical Approach
1. **Start with Brute Force**: Always explain the naive solution first
2. **Optimize Gradually**: Show your thought process for optimization
3. **Consider Edge Cases**: Discuss boundary conditions and error handling
4. **Time/Space Complexity**: Always analyze and state complexity
5. **Clean Code**: Write readable, well-structured code

### Communication Strategy
1. **Think Out Loud**: Verbalize your thought process
2. **Ask Clarifying Questions**: Understand requirements fully
3. **Explain Trade-offs**: Discuss different approaches and their pros/cons
4. **Test Your Solution**: Walk through examples and edge cases
5. **Handle Feedback**: Be receptive to hints and suggestions

### Gallup-Specific Considerations
- **Behavioral Questions**: Prepare STAR method responses
- **Leadership Examples**: Be ready to discuss technical leadership experiences
- **Problem-Solving Process**: Emphasize systematic approach to complex problems
- **Team Collaboration**: Highlight experience working with cross-functional teams
- **Continuous Learning**: Show commitment to staying current with technology
